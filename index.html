Brief

Business scenario: an operations team needs to understand why review scores declined while revenue continued to grow, and which issues should be investigated first. The short answer: revenue is concentrated in a handful of states and categories, negative reviews concentrate around one operational factor more than any other, and almost none of these customers come back. This is a self-directed portfolio project — the scenario is hypothetical and no real stakeholder commissioned it. Every relationship shown is observational: the data supports statements about association, not proof of cause. Every figure below was read out of the published Power BI measures, so this dashboard and the model cannot drift apart.
